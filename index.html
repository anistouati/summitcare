FAQ

Is SummitCare medical care?

No. SummitCare focuses on non-medical support: mobility, companionship, tech help, escort support, wellness coaching, and practical help at home.

Do clinics manage the workflow?

No. Clinics validate providers and earn commission. Families book providers directly. This keeps clinics out of operations while strengthening trust.

How does verification work?

Providers onboard, then clinics can verify providers they trust. Verified providers display a badge and may rank higher in discovery.

How are payments handled?

Payments are processed via Stripe. A completed booking triggers an automatic commission split between provider, clinic, and SummitCare.
