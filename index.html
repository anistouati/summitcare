FAQ

What kind of services are offered?

SummitCare focuses on non-medical support at home: tech help, mobility support, companionship, escort support, wellness coaching, and practical home help.

Is SummitCare medical care?

No. SummitCare is not a medical service. We focus on non-medical continuity support that helps seniors stay safe and supported at home.

How do providers join?

Providers can request access, complete onboarding, and publish their services and availability. Verification may apply depending on launch capacity.

How do clinics participate?

Clinics can partner during launch to validate trusted providers and offer families a safe path to non-medical support. We keep this lightweight.
